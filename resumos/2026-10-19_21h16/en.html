How to Sell Florida Real Estate to International Buyers

Summary

The training presents key guidelines for selling properties in Florida to international buyers, with a focus on the Brazilian market. It starts by clarifying the typical client profile, highlighting budget ranges and preferred locations.

The speaker explains that most Brazilian buyers look for properties priced between 400,000 and 800,000 dollars, mainly in areas near Orlando and Miami. This profile helps agents focus their prospecting and communication efforts.

The training also covers the main lead generation strategies, such as building partnerships with brokers based in Brazil and creating video content for social media to attract and educate potential clients.

Finally, it emphasizes the importance of serving clients in their own language. Providing service in the client’s native language significantly increases trust and, as a result, improves closing rates on property sales.
